Before the cake comes out

Put your phone on the table and press record.

Tell everyone the day before to bring one old photograph. Then put your phone on the table and press record before the cake comes out. Ask the questions one at a time and let everyone talk over each other, because that is where the good parts are. Say the date and who is at the table at the start, so that in thirty years somebody knows.

  1. 1

    Which relative from your childhood do you remember best, and why?

  2. 2

    Tell one family story that is vivid for you.

  3. 3

    Show your photograph. Who is on it, where was it taken, and when?

Preserve and cherish your family memories, while all the people who hold them are still sitting at the table.

After the holiday

Recorded something? Send it to us.

A recording in English, Hebrew, Russian or any mix of them is fine. So is a photograph of a page nobody in the family can read. You do not need to transcribe anything.

A Family Story Map

A memoir, a recording, or a folder of documents and photographs, placed back where it happened: an interactive map of every town your family lived in, with a family tree linked to it. Every place opens your own photograph and the part of the story that belongs to it.
