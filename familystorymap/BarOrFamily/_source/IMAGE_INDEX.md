## The four images that matter most for the map

- **18** — Chaya, Natan and Feigele in Kraków. The only picture of Zehava's father.
- **34** — Aunt Golda and her nephew wearing the star. The only wartime picture.
- **12** — Zehava's hand on the word WOLBROM at Yad Vashem.
- **24** — Zehava at six, the first photograph anyone ever took of her.
